The channels

Utopistopia

Short films about artificial intelligence

Each film takes one idea about where AI is heading and tells it as something that happens to one person — a doorbell, a loyalty card, a car. The dystopian half and the utopian half, and often the same machine. Every claim is checked against a named source: regulators, courts, campaign groups, labs, or the newsroom that broke it.

  • Under a minute, one story per film
  • Reported, not predicted — the source is named on screen
  • Eleven series in production, across books and news

Emberkit

One practical survival skill per film

Fire, water, shelter, knots, first aid, navigation. Each film teaches a single skill you can use with your hands, from a source you could go and check — a field manual, a first-aid body, or a book written by someone who has done it in the field.

  • About a minute, one skill per film
  • The source is named, so you can check it
  • Built from a 365-skill manual, one film per skill

How it's made

The films are produced by an in-house pipeline. A story is sourced and checked, a script is written and rewritten by hand, narration and artwork are generated, and the cut is assembled with burned-in captions and scored audio.

Every film goes through a human review before it is published: a reviewer watches the cut, leaves notes on the frames that are wrong, and an editor fixes them. Nothing is posted without a person approving it.

The publishing is handled by our own tool, which uploads finished films to TikTok as drafts for a person to check and publish. We post only films we have made and reviewed.
