Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Northlight ("we", "us") publishes short documentary films on social platforms. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

What we collect

When you visit this website, we do not collect personal information. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.

When you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you.

TikTok integration

Our publishing tool connects to TikTok through TikTok's official API. To upload and publish our own films, the tool stores an OAuth access token and refresh token for our TikTok account. These tokens are stored locally on our own systems and are never shared with third parties. They are used solely to upload and publish videos to our own TikTok account.

We do not collect, store, or process data belonging to TikTok users who view our films.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data (IP address, browser, timestamp) as part of normal web hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Data retention

Email correspondence is retained as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted. OAuth tokens are retained as long as the integration is active and deleted when it is revoked.

Your rights

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold about you by emailing hello@example.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do.

Contact
